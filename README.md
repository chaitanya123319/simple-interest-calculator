
# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned on a principal amount over a specified period of time.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- Principal: Initial amount of money
- Rate: Annual interest rate
- Time: Duration in years

## Example

Principal = 1000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Result = 100
